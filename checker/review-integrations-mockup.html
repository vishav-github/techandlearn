Client-wide defaults

Applies to every branch under this client unless changed later.

Fetch period
Only import reviews posted within this window. Reviews outside it are fetched but skipped — never imported or charged against your credit balance.
Auto-approve imported reviews
On: reviews count in reports immediately. Off: reviews land in the existing pending-approval queue for manual review first.
ⓘ No separate alert setting here — imported reviews are a normal Criticism, so alert rules are configured where all other alerts live (existing Alerts page), with "Google Imported" selectable as a condition just like any other source.

Where an imported review actually shows up

No new report screens — it lands as a normal evaluation record, so existing dashboards, KPI reports, and the approval queue already understand it.

Connected Review Accounts — company-wide, reused across any client

Modeled directly on the existing settings-whatsappaccounts.php screen: one company-wide list of connected external accounts, each independently OAuth-connected, tested, and reused. A client is never tied to an account by a field — the Discovery & Mapping step (per client) resolves that by matching each account's locations to branches.

How to connect a new account

  1. Google Business Profile: click "Connect new Google account" → Google's own OAuth consent screen → the signed-in Google user grants access to their Business Profile (or Organization) → Checker discovers every location under that grant in one step.
  2. Facebook Pages: click "Connect new Facebook account" → Facebook's OAuth/Business Manager consent screen, requesting pages_read_user_content → grants access to every Page in that Business Manager.
