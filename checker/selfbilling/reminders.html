Understanding Your Reminder Schedule

Our reminder system ensures you receive timely notifications for payments. This schedule counts **business days only** (Monday to Friday) from your invoice creation date, skipping weekends and public holidays.

Let's use an example: **Invoice created on July 1, 2025 (Tuesday).**

Event Business Days From Invoice Date Calendar Date (Example: Invoice 01/07/2025) What This Means
Invoice Creation Day 0 July 1, 2025 (Tuesday) Your starting point for all reminder calculations.
1st Reminder 4 Business Days After Invoice Date July 7, 2025 (Monday) We skip Saturday, July 5, and Sunday, July 6.
2nd Reminder 3 Business Days After 1st Reminder (Total: 7 Business Days) July 10, 2025 (Thursday) The gap from the 1st reminder is exactly 3 business days.
3rd Reminder 2 Business Days After 2nd Reminder (Total: 9 Business Days) July 14, 2025 (Monday) We skip Saturday, July 12, and Sunday, July 13.
4th Reminder 1 Business Day After 3rd Reminder (Total: 10 Business Days) July 15, 2025 (Tuesday) The gap from the 3rd reminder is exactly 1 business day.
Service Suspension 1 Business Day After 4th Reminder (Total: 11 Business Days) July 16, 2025 (Wednesday) Service will be suspended on this date if the invoice remains unpaid.

This table clearly demonstrates how each reminder and the suspension date align with your "business days only" rule and the precise 4, 3, 2, 1 day gaps.
