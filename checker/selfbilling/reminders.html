Understanding Your Reminder Schedule

Our reminder system ensures you receive timely notifications for payments. This schedule counts **business days only** (Monday to Friday) from your invoice creation date, skipping weekends and public holidays.

Let's use an example: **Invoice created on September 1, 2025 (Monday).**

📄

Invoice Creation

September 1, 2025 (Monday)
This is your starting point for all reminder calculations.
5 Business Days
📧

Gentle Reminder

September 8, 2025 (Monday)
Your invoice is due soon. This is a friendly reminder to process the payment.
5 Business Days
📧

Follow-up Reminder

September 15, 2025 (Monday)
This is a follow-up to our previous notification regarding your outstanding invoice.
4 Business Days
📧

Final Reminder

September 19, 2025 (Friday)
This is the final notification before your account is scheduled for suspension.
3 Business Days
⚠

Service Suspension

September 24, 2025 (Wednesday)
Service will be suspended on this date if the invoice remains unpaid.

This timeline provides a clear visual representation of your billing cycle, including weekends and the precise business day gaps for each reminder.
