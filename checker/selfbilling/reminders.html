Understanding Your Reminder Schedule

       

Our reminder system ensures you receive timely notifications for payments. This schedule counts **business days only** (Monday to Friday) from your invoice creation date, skipping weekends and public holidays.

       

Let's use an example: **Invoice created on September 1, 2025 (Monday).**
