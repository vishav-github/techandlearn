How each number is calculated (worked example: Reservation)

Benchmarking reuses the score the report already computes (FetchAverageGradeForCrits / AverageGrade, weighted by DChapterWeight, on the client's MinScore/MaxScore scale). It only adds the anonymised pool aggregate.

0 · Normalise to a 0–100 index

normalised = (score − MinScore) / (MaxScore − MinScore) × 100 — so clients on different scales compare like-for-like.

1 · Your score

The value already on the report, normalised. Your Reservation = 82.

2 · Market avg

Mean of the same unit across all other contributing branches in the group (you are excluded), read from the pre-computed snapshot. Peers 74, 79, 71, 80, 78 → (74+79+71+80+78)/5 = 76.4 → 76.

3 · vs Market

Your − Market avg. 82 − 76 = +6.

4 · Rank

1 + (peers with a strictly higher score), shown "rank of N". 82, 80, 79, 78, 74, 71 → rank 1 of 6.

5 · Top performer

Highest score in the pool for that unit, anonymised. 82.

6 · Trend (Phase 1.5)

(Your − Market)_now − (Your − Market)_prev. last +4, now +6 → ▲ +2. Needs two historical snapshots.

Highcharts appearance

Section & sub-section: a grouped Market column beside each You column (the reports already support a multi-series column chart). Final Score: a gauge with the needle at You and a market marker/band at the peer average. No new chart library — one extra series or plot-band plus tooltip fields.
