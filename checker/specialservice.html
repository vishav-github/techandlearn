Special Service Order Notifications

Single-page HTML mockup showing four notification templates for the special service workflow: agreement request, client confirmation to Checker team, billing confirmation after approval, and cancellation notice.

Workflow: Ticket → Agreement → Acceptance → Approval → Billing / Cancellation
Billing Model: Monthly Postpaid
Invoice Generation: 1st day of following month

1. Agreement Request

Notification from Checker team to the client requesting formal acceptance.

Checker → Client
Confirmation Required – Special Service Order Agreement

Dear [Client Name],

Thank you for choosing Checker’s expert services. Based on the requirements discussed in this support ticket:

We have prepared a Special Service Order for CSS Implementation with a fee of 35 EUR. This amount will be billed under the monthly postpaid billing cycle and included in the invoice generated on the 1st day of the following month.

📝
Action Required: Please review the agreement and click “I Agree” to authorize Checker to proceed.
I Agree

Once your agreement is received, the Checker team will proceed with the next internal review steps. If you need any clarification, please continue the discussion in the support ticket.

Best regards,
The Checker Team

2. Client Agreement Confirmed

Notification from the client to Checker team confirming agreement.

Client → Checker
Special Service Order Agreement Confirmed

Dear Checker Team,

We confirm the agreement for the Special Service Order as outlined in the support ticket. The request relates to CSS Implementation with an agreed fee of 35 EUR, which will be included in the next monthly postpaid invoice.

✅
Next Step: The request is now ready for Account Manager review and approval before scheduling.
Best regards,
Client Name

3. Billing Confirmation After Approval

Client-facing billing notice after the special service has been approved internally.

Client Email
Billing Information – Special Service Order

Dear [Client Name],

This is to confirm that Your Special Service Order for CSS Implementation has been approved and scheduled for execution.

The agreed fee of 35 EUR will be charged under the monthly postpaid billing cycle and included in the invoice generated on the 1st day of the following month.

💳
Billing Status: Approved for billing in the next monthly invoice cycle.

Our team will proceed with the requested work and will update you through the support ticket once the service has been completed.

Please note: Once the order has been approved and scheduled, cancellation is no longer available under the standard workflow.
Best regards,
The Checker Team

4. Cancellation Confirmation

Client-facing cancellation notice before internal approval.

Client Email
Special Service Order Cancellation Confirmation

Dear [Client Name],

We confirm that your Special Service Order for CSS Implementation with a fee of 35 EUR has been cancelled as per your request. As the cancellation was made before approval and scheduling, no charge will be applied.

❌
Billing Impact: This cancelled order will not appear on your monthly invoice.

If you would like to submit a revised request or proceed with a new service order, please continue the discussion through your support ticket.

Best regards,
The Checker Team

Mockup purpose only — placeholders such as client name, service name, amount, and ticket number can be dynamically populated in your system.
