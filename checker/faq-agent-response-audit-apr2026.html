FAQ10 In questionnaire settings, what is the meaning of: Convert the weights of questions into the weight of sections and sub-sections The setting "Convert the weights of questions into the weight of sections and sub-sections" in questionnaire settings determines how weights are distributed across sections and subsections. If set to "Always," the system will convert the weights of individual questions into the cumulative weight of sections and subsections, affecting the overall scoring logic. This can be useful for ensuring that the total weight reflects the intended scoring structure across different parts of the questionnaire. Conversely, if set to "Never," sections and subsections will retain their own specific weights, allowing for more granular control over how each part contributes to the final score. This setting is crucial for aligning the scoring results with your expectations and ensuring accurate weight distribution. ✅ Answered
